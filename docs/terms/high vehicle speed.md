# high vehicle speed

<div class="draft4-watermark" aria-hidden="true"></div>

[speed](speed.md) of a [vehicle](vehicle.md) that exceeds free flow motorway speeds by a factor of two or more

Clause: 3.7.5.12

EXAMPLE: A speed in excess of 198 km/h.

Note 1 to entry: Local regulations for the exact speed range vary.

History note: Introduced in ISO/TS 14812:2022
