# corporate customer model

<div class="draft4-watermark" aria-hidden="true"></div>

[contractual model](contractual model.md) where the [user](user.md) is a [commercial](commercial.md) [entity](entity.md)

Clause: 3.5.7.2

Example 1: A business purchasing monthly [public transport](public transport.md) passes for its employees.

Example 2: A commercial entity using a commercial courier service for package delivery.
