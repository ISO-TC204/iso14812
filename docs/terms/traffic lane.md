# traffic lane

<div class="draft4-watermark" aria-hidden="true"></div>

portion of [carriageway](carriageway.md) designed to accommodate a single line of moving [road vehicles](road vehicle.md)

    <img alt="traffic lane Diagram" src="../../diagrams/traffic lane.dot.png" /> <!-- Fallback for non-SVG browsers -->
</object>

Clause: 3.3.1.13

Alternative preferred term: lane

Note 1 to entry: A traffic lane can be bi-directional, such as a single lane [road](road.md), a two-way turn/overtaking lane, or a reversible flow lane.

Note 2 to entry: Some jurisdictions allow supplemental uses of a traffic lane, such as multiple motorcycles sharing the width of a traffic lane or allowing a bicycle to use the edge of a lane.

Note 3 to entry: The term "lane" is often used to refer to a "traffic lane" when the context is known to be "traffic".

History note: Introduced in ISO/TS 14812:2022
